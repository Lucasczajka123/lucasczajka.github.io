About

I'm the founder of Parametric (YC F25), where we build bimanual robots for repetitive manipulation work in warehouses — starting with autonomous box folding for fulfillment operations.

Before this, Parametric began as a physical evaluation platform for robotics: standardized, versioned real-world environments for measuring what robots can actually do outside of sim. That obsession with measurement never left — we run a teleoperation data collection team in Kigali and spend a lot of time on grading protocols, inter-rater reliability, and what it takes to make a robot demo repeatable.

Interests: vision-language-action models, imitation learning, the sim-to-real gap, and the economics of physical AI.
